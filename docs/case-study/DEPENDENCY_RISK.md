They require framework-level upgrade planning and per-target reachability review rather
than a force update during this containment release.

## Gate policy

- Production high/critical npm findings fail CI.
- Full npm and Rust reports remain visible even when a finding is accepted.
- Accepted findings require a scope explanation, compensating control, owner, and
  review date.
- A force upgrade is not considered remediation until the affected build and workflow
  tests pass.
- Any new production high/critical finding blocks publication unless it has a written,
  time-bounded exception and evidence that the vulnerable path is unreachable.
